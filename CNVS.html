CNVS

The Closed Native Verification Systems (CNVS) Theory provides the theoretical framework for the Golden Nexus Protocol. Below are the links to the documents, along with their respective generated hashes and proofs of publication.




This documents defines a Class of Systems based on Native Verification.
It is independent from any specific implementation.
Native Verification is the Condition in which an Object originates
only upon the Proof of its Existence.
Consequently the Term "Native Verification" denotes physical Origin as evidence resulting from Objective Verification.

The Closed Native Verification Systems (CNVS) Theory comprises:

- Three Axioms

- The law of state transition

- The Principle of Ignorance

- The Theorem of Emergent Security.

Golden Protocol Nexus is one possible implementation of this theoretical framework.
