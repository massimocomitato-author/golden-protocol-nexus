Editorial Note on Formalization: The MTC/ LEAN 4 models provided in this version represent an early-stage, exploratory prototype. They are intended as a preliminary proof of concept to illustrate the foundational intuition of the theory. Consequently, these formalizations may contain simplifications, local tautologies, or oversights. A comprehensive, generalized, and rigorous revision of the codebase is currently underway and will be deployed in a future update.
