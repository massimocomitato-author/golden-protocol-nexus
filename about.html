About Golden Protocol Nexus

Golden Protocol Nexus is a theoretical and architectural framework for a verification-native financial system.

The system is designed to eliminate trust assumptions by replacing them with continuous verification, cryptographic proof, and deterministic protocol rules.

All documents published on this site are part of an evolving research process. Each version is independently verifiable through cryptographic hashing and Bitcoin timestamping.

The protocol is developed as an open conceptual system, where transparency, verifiability, and logical consistency are fundamental principles.

— Massimo Comitato
