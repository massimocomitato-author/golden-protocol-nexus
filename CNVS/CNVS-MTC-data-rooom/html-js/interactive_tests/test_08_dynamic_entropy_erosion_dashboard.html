CNVS Interactive Stress Test: Dynamic Erosion of Residual Min-Entropy

Mathematical Model (Heuristic Erosion): This dashboard acts as an interactive stress test. It projects the Systemic Reconstruction Probability assuming that the expansion of colluding nodes (q) causes a dynamic depletion of the cryptographic Min-Entropy. The three curves model increasingly severe degradation profiles (Linear, Quadratic, Radical). The interface illustrates the sensitivity of reconstruction probability to critical-fragment cardinality (m) under severe heuristic metadata-leakage assumptions.
