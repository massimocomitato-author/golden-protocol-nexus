Protocol Flow

Two execution paths, one final condition: issuance occurs only when protocol state is valid.

External Issuer Path

Used during bootstrap or VRS expansion, when new physical assets enter the system.
1. Buyer Locks Funds The buyer submits an order and funds are locked in protocol-controlled escrow.
↓
2. Physical Seller / Issuer Enters A real-world seller provides gold not yet included in the Verified Reserve State.
↓
3. Asset Acquisition or Provision The issuer either acquires gold externally or provides pre-owned gold not yet registered in VRS.
↓
4. Protocol-Assigned Transport The protocol assigns transport and custody routing. Direct actor coordination is minimized.
↓
5. Vault Entry and Audit The asset enters vault custody. Auditors verify physical existence, quantity, and consistency.
↓
6. Validator Confirmation Validators verify certificates, signatures, shard references, and protocol state consistency.
↓
7. VRS Expansion The verified asset expands the Verified Reserve State. Only after validation may escrow be released.

Internal VRS-Native Path

Used after VRS exists, when exchange occurs through verified ownership reallocation.
1. Buyer Locks Funds The buyer submits an order and funds are locked until protocol completion.
↓
2. Seller Holds VRS Ownership The seller already owns verified VRS-backed ownership inside the protocol.
↓
3. Order sharding Buyer and seller orders are fragmented into execution shards.
↓
4. Non-Linear Matching Buyer shards are matched with seller shards. Matching is many-to-many and protocol-driven.
↓
5. Auditor Verification Auditors verify that the relevant VRS and custody references remain consistent.
↓
6. Validator Confirmation Validators verify ownership state, signatures, shard integrity, and invariant compliance.
↓
7. Ownership Reallocation No physical movement occurs. Verified ownership is reassigned through protocol state transition.
Common Final Condition
I(si) = 1 if and only if Valid(si)
Issuance is not path-dependent.
It is condition-dependent.

No ownership is generated unless custody, audit, validation, and protocol invariants are satisfied.
External issuance expands VRS. Internal execution reallocates verified ownership. Both paths converge into the same deterministic protocol rule.
